# Royal Haven V1527: permanent market kiosks

Two original, realistic merchant assets replace the repetitive southern cloth-awning stalls: a square-timber shingle-roof spice kiosk and a stone-based terracotta-roof pottery booth. Their original market lots, width/height, orientation and collision radii are preserved. Proportional fitting produces measured counter heights of 51.3 and 53.2 world units. Two faded angular flagstone aprons are baked into existing terrain tiles. No extra ground draw, new collider, NPC animation, or camera change. Artwork was generated with the built-in image generation tool, cropped and scaled to the existing play-space aspect ratio, and stored as efficient WebP.

V1527 starts from the exact browser-tested V1526 HTML. The browser test verifies artwork readiness, unchanged market geometry, existing solid footprints, apron metadata, and all earlier district checks.
